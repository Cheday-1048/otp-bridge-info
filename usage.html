Usage information

Updated 21 October 2026

Authorized accounts only

Connect only Gmail accounts you own or have explicit permission to manage. Account owners grant access on Google’s own authorization page; the bot does not require their Google passwords.

Choose the destination carefully

Verification codes can allow access to an account. Only send them to a Telegram chat whose members are authorized to receive them. The administrator controls the sender allowlist and destination.

Availability

Delivery depends on the bot’s host, network connection, Google authorization and Gmail and Telegram availability. Messages can be delayed, missed or expire. The tool does not guarantee delivery or permanent Google access.

Stopping access

Account owners can revoke access in their Google Account. Contact the administrator who connected your account for support, local data removal or changes to forwarding.
