A managed email notification tool

Verification codes, in your Telegram chat.

OTP Bridge connects Gmail accounts that their owners explicitly authorize. It checks recent messages and forwards matching verification codes from a configured list of trusted senders to the Telegram chat chosen by the administrator.

How it works

  1. The account owner grants read-only Gmail access through Google.
  2. The bot checks recent messages on a privately operated computer or server.
  3. Matching codes arrive in the configured Telegram chat, labelled with the original Gmail account, sender and message time.

Managed account connections

The administrator connects each mailbox with the account owner’s explicit authorization. Account owners choose to share matching verification codes with the configured Telegram destination. Only connect accounts you own or are authorized to manage.

Account control

You can revoke the app’s access from your Google Account at any time. Contact the administrator who connected your account for help or removal of locally stored authorization and delivery records.
